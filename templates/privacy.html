Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

BEL Transportation NYC LLC ("RideBEL," "we," "us," or "our") operates ridebel.com. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or contact us through it.

Our website currently accepts general inquiries. Transportation services are not yet available. Please do not submit medical records, diagnoses, insurance or Medicaid numbers, Social Security numbers, payment information, or other sensitive personal information through this form. The form is not for emergencies or booking transportation.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, reason for contacting us, and message. You may also choose to provide a phone number and organization. We receive the information you include in any follow-up correspondence.

When you browse the site, our hosting and security systems may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and request times. We also use limited technical records to help prevent repeated or abusive submissions.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to answer questions, communicate with you about your inquiry, and handle related business follow-up. We use technical information to operate the website, troubleshoot problems, prevent spam and misuse, and protect the site. We may also retain or use information as needed to meet legal obligations or address disputes.

Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter or marketing mailing list.

Email delivery and service providers

Contact-form submissions are sent to our business email inbox. They are not stored as contact-message records in the website's application database, but copies may remain in our email account, related correspondence, and provider backups.

We use service providers, including Google for hosting and business email, to support these functions. Those providers process information in connection with the services they provide. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or when you direct or authorize us to do so.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses essential security cookies to help protect form submissions. Administrative functions may use additional essential cookies. Blocking these cookies in your browser may prevent some features from working.

We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tools on this website.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry correspondence while it is needed to respond, complete related follow-up, or maintain necessary business or legally required records. There is no single retention period for every inquiry. Technical security records and provider backups may have different retention periods.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use the form and can leave the optional phone and organization fields blank. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided, or to ask us to stop nonessential follow-up. We may need to verify your request and may retain information for legal obligations, security, or other legitimate recordkeeping needs. Rights available under applicable law remain unaffected.

Security

We use HTTPS and access controls to help protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please limit your message to information needed for a general inquiry.

Children's information

Our contact form is intended for general inquiries from adults. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Links to other websites

Links, including our website developer's credit, may take you to other websites. Their operators have their own privacy practices. This policy applies to ridebel.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Any additional notice or consent required by applicable law will be provided.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact BEL Transportation NYC LLC at erojas@ridebel.com. Please do not include sensitive personal information in an initial privacy request.
