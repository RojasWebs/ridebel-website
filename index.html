Non-Emergency Medical Transportation

Who We Are

BEL Transportation NYC LLC was founded by three experienced professional drivers with approximately 30 years of combined experience in passenger transportation. We believe transportation should be more than just getting from one place to another—it should be safe, reliable, respectful, and dependable.

Our focus is on serving our community with care, professionalism, and the kind of personal attention every passenger deserves.

Our Mission

RideBEL's mission is to provide safe, reliable, accessible, and respectful transportation that helps people stay connected to medical care and other essential services throughout New York City.

We are committed to serving our community—including older adults, people with disabilities, and individuals with mobility needs—with dignity, compassion, professionalism, and personal attention.

Our Planned Services

RideBEL is preparing to provide safe, reliable, and accessible transportation throughout New York City.

Ambulatory Transportation

Planned transportation services for passengers who can enter and exit the vehicle without wheelchair assistance.

Wheelchair-Accessible Transportation

Planned accessible transportation for passengers who use wheelchairs and require appropriately equipped vehicles.

Medical & Essential Appointments
